# F4F Checker privacy policy

Last updated October 9, 2026. Covers F4F Checker 1.0.7.

F4F Checker compares the following and follower lists shown in your signed-in Instagram session to identify accounts you follow that do not follow you back. The comparison runs in your browser when you start a check.

## Information used

The extension reads your Instagram username, follower and following counts, and the usernames and profile links in those lists. These can identify you and other people. It reads relevant page elements to find the profile and list controls, opens the lists and scrolls them to load entries. It also reads the current Instagram page URL to check which profile is open. It does not maintain a general browsing-history database or log your keystrokes or mouse movements.

It uses this information to collect and validate the lists, compare them, show progress and display the results. It does not automatically follow or unfollow accounts, like posts or send messages.

You sign in directly on Instagram. The extension does not ask for your password, read password values or access authentication cookies. It detects whether the page requires sign-in so it can ask you to complete it on Instagram.

## Local storage and retention

During a check, list entries are held in the page's memory. The extension keeps operation status, your username, account names and profile links in the result, counts, validation details, timestamps and tab identifiers in browser session storage. This lets progress and results remain available when you close and reopen the popup. Closing the popup does not erase them. Session storage is cleared when the browser session ends or the extension reloads; starting another check replaces the current operation. A result displayed in an Instagram page can remain there until the page is reloaded or closed.

Your appearance preference is saved separately in local extension storage and remains between browser sessions. Uninstalling the extension removes its extension storage through the browser.

## Connections and sharing

The extension has no developer-operated server, analytics or direct network client. It does not upload follower lists, results or page content to the developer. Its clicks and scrolling cause Instagram's own website to load data through your normal signed-in connection. Opening a result link visits that profile on Instagram. Instagram operates independently and its own terms and privacy policy apply.

The extension does not sell user data, transfer it for unrelated purposes, use it for advertising or use it to determine creditworthiness or make lending decisions. Information read by the extension is used only for the list-comparison feature and its controls.

## Your controls and support

You choose when to start a check and can cancel it. Use the tool on your own account and keep other people's information private. Avoid sharing result screenshots or account lists without considering the privacy of the people they identify.

If you open a support issue, you choose what information to send. GitHub issues are public. Do not include passwords, private account lists or other sensitive information.
